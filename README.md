# Circassian-Mirror

## Story

One day I accidentally came across this mirror on Pinterest. I was immediately drawn to its shape, ornamentation, and gold embroidery. There was something surprisingly calm and beautiful about it. Some time later, I contacted the artisan who had created it and purchased it because I genuinely liked it.

This mirror has no practical value to me. Rather, it is a small object that reminds me of the beauty of human culture and of how much work, talent, and memory can be contained within a single thing.

Sometimes I think that one day I may give it to a girl, if there is ever someone in my life with whom I share friendship, trust, and a warm human connection. It seems to me that beautiful things should continue their story alongside people.

## About Circassian Gold Embroidery

Circassian gold embroidery is one of the most renowned traditions of the decorative and applied arts of the Caucasus. For many generations, artisans created intricate ornaments using gold and silver threads, decorating ceremonial clothing, headdresses, belts, and household objects.

Each pattern reflects not only technical mastery but also the cultural memory of a people. The ornaments often include floral motifs, geometric compositions, and elements passed down from generation to generation. Even small pieces frequently required many hours of meticulous work.

Today, this art continues to be preserved and revived by artisans who treat their heritage with great care and respect.

## Why This Repository Exists

I am engaged in scientific research and spend a great deal of time reading not only about mathematics, biology, and physics, but also about the history and cultures of different peoples. The more one learns about the world, the more one realizes how remarkable humanity is in its diversity.

For me, science does not have value in itself. Its value lies in its ability to help people. And people create languages, music, traditions, crafts, works of art, and cultural heritage such as this small mirror.

For that reason, this repository preserves more than photographs of an object. It preserves a reminder of whom science ultimately serves and why it is worth continuing research even when the path becomes long and difficult.
